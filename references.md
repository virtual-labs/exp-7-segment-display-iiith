### Textbooks

**1. Digital Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano and Michael Ciletti - Chapter 4 (Combinational Logic)<br/>
_Comprehensive coverage of decoders, BCD to 7-segment converters, and display interface circuits._

**2. Digital Logic and Computer Design**<br/>
BY: Morris Mano - Chapter 4<br/>
_Fundamental concepts of combinational logic design including decoder circuits and display systems._

**3. Digital Electronics: Principles, Devices and Applications**<br/>
BY: Anil K. Maini - Chapter 7<br/>
_Practical approach to decoder design with detailed analysis of 7-segment display systems._

**4. Digital Systems: Principles and Applications**<br/>
BY: Ronald J. Tocci, Neal S. Widmer, and Gregory L. Moss - Chapter 9<br/>
_Excellent coverage of BCD systems, display decoders, and interface circuits._
